BUILT FROM A REAL FRUSTRATION

It began with a student who already knew how to plan.

Between lessons, deadlines, assignments and personal projects, the day was carefully organised.

But one delayed meeting could turn the rest of it into a chain of decisions. What should move? What should stay? What could wait?

Existing productivity apps helped organise tasks and calendars. But when reality disrupted the plan, the hardest part remained: figuring out how to reorganise everything that followed.

Amono is being built to solve that problem.

A few things you might be wondering.

What exactly is Amono?+

Amono is an adaptive calendar and decision engine being developed to help you manage the moving parts of everyday life — so you can focus on what needs doing, not on constantly organising everything around it.

Is Amono available yet?+

Amono is currently in development. Join the waitlist to help shape it, follow its progress and receive early-access updates.

What happens after I join the waitlist?+

We want early members to help shape Amono by sharing feedback and influencing what we build next. You’ll also receive development updates and information about early access.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?+

Joining the waitlist is free. No payment is required to register.

Will Amono make changes without my approval?+

You remain in control. The intended experience is for Amono to suggest a change, explain why it makes sense and let you approve, reject or explore alternatives.
