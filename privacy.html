Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Amono is an independent project in development. This notice explains what information is collected when you join the Amono waitlist, why it is used and how you can ask for it to be changed or deleted.

1. Who is responsible?

The waitlist is operated under the name Amono, an independent project that is not currently incorporated as a company. The person operating the project is responsible for the waitlist information. For privacy questions or requests, contact amono.calendar@gmail.com.

2. What information is collected?

When you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter and a record of your email-update consent. Netlify, our website hosting and form-processing provider, may also process technical information connected with a visit or submission, such as an IP address, browser or device details, timestamps and security logs.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the waitlist form. It only asks for an email address.

3. Why do we use your information?

We use your email address to manage the Amono waitlist and, where you have actively agreed, to send messages about Amono’s development, waitlist news and early access. We may also use relevant submission and technical information to prevent spam, protect the website and resolve form issues.

4. Legal basis

We rely on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR to use your email address for the waitlist communications described above. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing amono.calendar@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Our hosting and form provider may separately process technical data as needed to operate, maintain and secure its services under its own contractual terms and privacy documentation.

5. Who receives the information?

We use Netlify to host the website and receive and store waitlist form submissions. Netlify processes form data on behalf of the website operator. We do not sell or rent waitlist email addresses, and we do not give them to advertisers for their own marketing.

This website also loads typefaces using Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries outside the United Kingdom, including the United States. Netlify describes its international transfer arrangements in its Privacy Statement and Data Processing Agreement, including the use of recognised contractual safeguards where required. Google’s processing is described in its privacy documentation linked above.

7. How long is information kept?

We keep your email address while you remain on the waitlist and while it is needed to provide the updates you requested. We will remove it if you withdraw consent or ask us to delete it, and when the waitlist closes and the information is no longer needed. We will review inactive entries at least once every 12 months and delete or anonymise information that is no longer required, unless we must retain a limited record for legal or security reasons.

8. Your choices and rights

You can withdraw consent or ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete information, where the law allows;
  • restrict or object to certain processing; or
  • provide your information in a portable format, where that right applies.

To make a request, email amono.calendar@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

9. Cookies and analytics

The current landing page does not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts as described above. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking tools are added later, this notice will be updated and any required choices will be provided.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect waitlist information and limit access to it. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so please only submit the email address requested by the form.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the waitlist or the way the website works changes. The latest version will always be available on this page, with its revision date shown at the top.
